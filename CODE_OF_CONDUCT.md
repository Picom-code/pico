# Code of conduct

Be kind, assume good faith, and keep critique about the code, not the person.
Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, PRs, discussions or any other project space.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and block participants who don't follow this.
Report problems privately to the maintainers through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/Picom-code/pico/security/advisories/new) or by contacting the repo owner.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
