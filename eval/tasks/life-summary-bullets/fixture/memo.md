# Memo: Riverside library renovation — status update

From: Dana Okafor, facilities lead
To: Library board

The renovation of the Riverside branch is now about 60% complete. The new children's wing has its
roof and windows in, and electrical work starts next week.

Budget: the board approved an extra $48,000 on Tuesday to cover the asbestos removal discovered in
the basement in August. That brings the total project budget to $1.23 million.

Schedule: because of the asbestos work, the reopening deadline moves from 1 December to 2 February.
The contractor (Halvorsen Build) has confirmed this date in writing.

Services: the pop-up library at the community centre stays open until the reopening, Monday to
Saturday, 10:00–18:00. Book returns can be made at either the pop-up or the Main Street branch.

Volunteers: we need 12 volunteers for the move-back weekend (late January). Sign-up sheet is at the
pop-up desk.

Risks: the furniture supplier warned that shelving could arrive two weeks late; we're asking for a
partial early delivery of the children's wing shelving.

Next board update: 15 November.
